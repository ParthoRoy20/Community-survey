Participant Information & Consent

Revitalising Rural Retail: Community Shopping & Service Improvement Survey

1. Aim of the Study:
This research study investigates local grocery shopping habits, online delivery usage, and community interest in new retail and social hub services (such as a café or ready-to-eat food counter) at the Micheldever Village Store.

2. Voluntary Participation & Time Commitment:
Your participation is entirely voluntary and takes approximately 3 to 5 minutes. You may stop and exit the questionnaire at any point before submitting without consequence.

3. Complete Anonymity & Withdrawal:
This survey is strictly anonymous. No names, personal email addresses, phone numbers, or IP addresses are tracked or stored. Because responses cannot be linked back to individual participants, data cannot be modified or withdrawn once submitted.

4. Data Handling & Confidentiality:
All gathered information will be stored securely on a password-protected academic drive in strict accordance with the Data Protection Act (2018). Data will only be reported in aggregate within an academic postgraduate dissertation.
